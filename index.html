Play money only. Dollars here have no cash value. Sharps Exchange is a private game for friends and family and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL or any team. Player names and photos belong to their owners.

How it works

  1. Start with $10,000. Buy shares of any player or team defense at the listed price.
  2. Prices move weekly. After each week's games, real results move everyone's prices at once. Good games lift a price and bad games drop it.
  3. Earn dividends. Each bonus point a player scores pays you 1% of the share price, per share held.
  4. Sell any time. No fees. Compare your net worth on the League tab.
Player stats come from public nflverse data. Game dollars ($) have no cash value.
